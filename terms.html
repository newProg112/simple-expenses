Terms for using Simple Books

These draft terms describe the intended service and current product behaviour. They are not final contractual terms or certified legal advice. The unresolved items must be decided and legally reviewed before publication.

Draft date: 2 September 2026

1. Provider and status of this draft

Proposed provider: Adam Elvin trading as Simple Books. This identity is awaiting owner confirmation. Contact: adam@simple-books.co.uk.

Publication blocker: confirm the legal identity, publishable address and any required registration details. These terms must not be presented as binding until the owner and an appropriately qualified reviewer have approved the unresolved provisions.

2. The service and intended audience

Simple Books is web-based business management software for tasks such as customer and supplier records, invoices, bills, expenses, mileage, projects, budgets, banking records, reports, exports and optional AI-assisted features.

The product is designed principally for freelancers, sole traders and small owner-managed businesses. That design intention does not by itself determine whether a particular user is legally a business customer or consumer, and nothing in these draft terms is intended to remove rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Simple Books is not an accountant, tax adviser, lawyer, financial adviser or regulated payment provider. The software, calculations, reports, forecasts, guides and AI outputs are informational tools and are not professional advice. Users remain responsible for their records, decisions, filings, tax treatment and obtaining advice where needed.

3. Accounts and user responsibilities

  • Users must provide accurate account information and keep it reasonably current.
  • Users are responsible for protecting their password and devices and for activity carried out through their account, except where the law provides otherwise.
  • Users must have authority to enter business records and upload files, including personal information, confidential material and intellectual property belonging to other people.
  • The shared Demo must contain sample information only. Users must not enter real business or personal information into it.
  • Users should promptly report suspected unauthorised access or security problems.

Owner confirmation required: decide whether an account age requirement is needed and, if so, obtain legal advice on the wording and verification approach.

4. Starter and Pro

Starter is the current free plan. It can be opened without entering a payment card and includes the published Starter limits and features.

Pro is intended to cost £15 GBP per month and includes the published Pro limits and features. Checkout and subscription management take place through Stripe. Features and usage limits are described on the current Pricing page.

Publication blocker: confirm whether £15 is inclusive or exclusive of VAT, whether VAT will be charged, and how the price must be displayed for each customer type. No VAT treatment is asserted in this draft.

5. Subscription billing, renewal and cancellation

A Pro subscription is billed monthly by Stripe and is intended to renew each month until cancelled. Stripe displays the payment amount and payment details during checkout. The subscriber is responsible for keeping a valid payment method where payment is due.

Eligible subscriptions can be managed through the Stripe billing portal linked from the Account page. When cancellation is scheduled for the end of the current billing period, Pro access remains available while Stripe continues to report the subscription as active or trialing. Access ends when the subscription becomes cancelled or otherwise ceases to qualify. Failed, incomplete, unpaid, paused or other non-active billing states may remove Pro access, while portal access may remain available to resolve billing.

This draft does not create a refund, credit, proration or cooling-off policy. Any mandatory statutory rights continue to apply.

Publication blocker: decide and legally review refund, failed-payment, price-change, cancellation-notice and consumer cancellation/cooling-off treatment. Confirm what Stripe Checkout and the portal will display before publication.

6. Customer content and permissions

As between the user and Simple Books, users retain their rights in business records and files they submit. Users give Simple Books the limited permission needed to host, copy, transmit, process, display and delete that content for operating, securing and supporting the service and carrying out their requests.

Users are responsible for the accuracy and legality of their content and for giving appropriate notices to, and obtaining any necessary permissions from, people whose information they record. Separate data-processing terms may be required where Simple Books processes personal information on a customer’s behalf.

7. Acceptable use

Users must not:

  • use Simple Books unlawfully, fraudulently or to infringe another person’s rights;
  • upload malicious code or material they are not authorised to use;
  • try to gain unauthorised access to another account, provider, system or data;
  • interfere with security controls, service operation or reasonable usage limits;
  • use automated access in a way that damages or overloads the service; or
  • use AI features to submit unlawful content or sensitive information that is not necessary for the requested task.

Reasonable security restrictions may be applied to prevent abuse or protect users and providers. Broader suspension or termination rights require owner and legal confirmation.

8. AI-assisted features

The AI Assistant and document scanner are optional. Relevant information is sent to OpenAI through Firebase Functions when a user invokes them, as described in the Privacy Policy draft.

AI output may be incomplete, inaccurate or unsuitable. Users must review questions, extracted fields, figures and suggested text before relying on them. Scanning a document does not by itself save a bill or expense, and the AI Assistant does not create, edit or delete business records. AI output is not accounting, tax, legal or financial advice.

9. Exports, backups and portability

Simple Books provides user-controlled downloads for supported records. Users should make and securely retain their own appropriate backups.

Exports are not complete copies of everything held by the service. Depending on the format, they omit some attachments and logos, authentication credentials, billing and subscription data, operational or administrative records, generated reports, and parts of payment or settlement history. A downloaded workbook or JSON file may therefore be insufficient on its own to recreate every state in an account.

Simple Books is not presented as an automatic backup service, disaster-recovery guarantee or permanent archive. Users should open and check important exports rather than assume a download is complete or restorable.

10. Account deletion

The Account page provides an irreversible deletion workflow after recent reauthentication. Users should export anything they need before starting it. The implemented workflow cancels a linked Simple Books subscription, removes uploaded files and Simple Books account data, and deletes the Firebase Authentication account. The user may lose access before any unresolved support issue is completed.

Deletion from Simple Books does not necessarily erase transaction, security, legal or backup records independently retained by service providers. Further details and unresolved retention decisions are identified in the Privacy Policy draft.

11. Availability and changes

The service may be interrupted by maintenance, defects, internet availability or third-party providers. No uptime or recovery service level is promised in this draft.

Features, limits and plans may need to change as the product develops, but the process for material service or contractual changes, advance notice and treatment of existing subscribers has not yet been approved.

Owner confirmation required: approve fair change-notice, service-suspension and termination provisions, including how users can export data and how paying subscribers are treated.

12. Warranties and liability

No final warranty disclaimer, liability allocation or financial cap has been selected. Nothing in the final terms should exclude or limit liability or statutory rights where doing so would be unlawful.

Publication blocker: obtain legal advice and approve proportionate warranty, remedy, indemnity and liability provisions suitable for both the service and any users who may legally be consumers. A liability cap has deliberately not been invented.

13. Governing law and disputes

No governing law, court jurisdiction or formal dispute process has been selected in this draft.

Publication blocker: decide these points with legal advice, taking account of the operator’s location, target market and any mandatory consumer protections.

14. Contact

Questions about these draft terms can be sent to adam@simple-books.co.uk.
