Why Simple Books exists

Bring the pieces of a small business together

Simple Books started as a practical project focused on making everyday business records easier to organise and understand.

For many small businesses, useful information ends up spread across invoices, bills, receipts, spreadsheets and separate files. Simple Books brings those records and the tools around them into one place, so it is easier to see what is happening and decide what needs attention.

The aim is not to make people learn accounting software for its own sake. It is to make the information behind a business more manageable.

Invoices Bills and receipts Expenses and mileage Projects and budgets Reports and exports

Connected records are easier to follow than a collection of separate files.

Working with an accountant

Stay organised between conversations with your accountant

Simple Books can sit alongside professional support. Keeping structured records in one place can make it easier to review the business and prepare useful information to share.

Reports, exports and the Accountant Pack can help you gather relevant records. Simple Books does not replace professional judgement.

Simple Books is business management software and is not a substitute for professional financial or tax advice.
