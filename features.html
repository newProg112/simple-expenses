AI-assisted features

Practical help with your own records

AI is used for focused tasks inside Simple Books, with you staying in control of what is saved.

AI

Ask about your business data

The AI Assistant answers questions about your Simple Books invoices, bills, expenses, projects, budgets and cashflow. It explains the records already in your account; it does not create, edit or delete them.

SCAN

Spend less time retyping documents

Scan a supplier bill or expense receipt to prepare useful details for review. You choose whether to apply them and check the original before saving the record.

Banking

Bring CSV bank statements into view

Import a CSV bank statement, map the columns and review transactions alongside your existing records. Simple Books can suggest possible matches to invoices, bills and expenses, helping you work through your statement more quickly.

This is statement import and review—not a live Open Banking feed or completed reconciliation workflow.
