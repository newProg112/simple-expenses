Data ownership

Your business. Your records. Your copy.

We think you should keep copies of your own business records—even if you have no intention of leaving Simple Books.

Trust the software. Keep your own copy anyway.

Use the supported workbook structure to prepare records, import them when appropriate, and export supported business records back to Excel. Keep those files on your own computer or storage as part of your normal routine.

One of the first things we’d encourage you to try: create a test invoice, export your workbook and open it yourself.

Supported workbook journey

Excel workbook ↔ Simple Books

The canonical workbook supports Clients, Invoices, Invoice Items, Bills, Expenses, Mileage, Projects and Budgets.

Fill→Import→Use→Export

The workbook covers supported business records, not every part of Simple Books. The exported Summary is not imported, and Banking, attachments and logos, payment or settlement history and generated reports are excluded. Paid invoices and bills need their missing payment history before they can be recreated as paid.

Deal with it while it is in front of you

You don’t have to sit down and “do the books”.

Use Simple Books in a mobile browser and choose a JPG, PNG, WEBP or PDF bill or expense document from your device. AI scanning can suggest possible details for you to review.

You decide whether to use those suggestions. Saving the record remains a separate, deliberate action.
