How this is calculated

Insights are calculated each time from your Simple Books invoices, bills, expenses and mileage, projects, budgets, and validated accounting journals. Revenue, expenses and profit use the same journal-based approach as Profit & Loss; outstanding amounts, budgets and project margins use their corresponding operational records.

Actionable Insights use current-month source records for customer and expense attribution, source-record project profitability, reliable paid dates, upcoming bill due dates, and validated VAT Input and VAT Output journal lines. The VAT position is an estimate based on recorded Simple Books transactions and must be reviewed before filing.

The health score starts at 60 and applies capped adjustments for overdue invoices, revenue and expense movement, profitability, project performance and budget pressure. Scores are limited to 0–100: 80–100 Strong, 60–79 Healthy, 40–59 Needs attention, and 0–39 At risk.

No AI is used. These insights are informational and are not professional financial, tax or legal advice.
