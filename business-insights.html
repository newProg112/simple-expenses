Forecasts & Projections

Forward-looking estimates calculated from your recorded Simple Books activity.

How forecasts are calculated
  • Month-end revenue and expenses extend validated month-to-date journal totals at their daily average across all calendar days in the month. Profit is projected revenue less projected expenses.
  • Comparisons use the same elapsed calendar days in the previous month, never a previous full month.
  • Budget projections extend recorded spend across the budget’s inclusive date range when a meaningful part of that range has elapsed.
  • Payment behaviour requires at least two reliably dated paid invoices and uses recorded due dates and current overdue invoices.
  • VAT is estimated from current-period VAT Output account 2100 less VAT Input account 1200. It is not a VAT return and must be reviewed before filing.
  • Cash outlook is unavailable because Simple Books does not currently store both an authoritative opening bank balance and reliable settlement movements.

Forecasts are estimates, not guarantees or professional financial, tax or legal advice. No AI is used.

How this is calculated

Insights are calculated each time from your Simple Books invoices, bills, expenses and mileage, projects, budgets, and validated accounting journals. Revenue, expenses and profit use the same journal-based approach as Profit & Loss; outstanding amounts, budgets and project margins use their corresponding operational records.

Actionable Insights use current-month source records for customer and expense attribution, source-record project profitability, reliable paid dates, upcoming bill due dates, and validated VAT Input and VAT Output journal lines. The VAT position is an estimate based on recorded Simple Books transactions and must be reviewed before filing.

The health score starts at 60 and applies capped adjustments for overdue invoices, revenue and expense movement, profitability, project performance and budget pressure. Scores are limited to 0–100: 80–100 Strong, 60–79 Healthy, 40–59 Needs attention, and 0–39 At risk.

No AI is used. These insights are informational and are not professional financial, tax or legal advice.
